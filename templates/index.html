Frequently asked questions.

How can I sell my books?
You can visit the nearest ReLove bookstore listed in the website, sell your book for cash or swap it for a preloved book for your choice.
How can I sell my clothes?
You can visit the nearest ReLove clothing store listed in the website, sell your clothes per kg and receive ReLove points.
What can I do with my ReLove points?
You can visit the nearest ReLove store and receive any items eligible for that amount of points.
How do collection points work?
Second hand clothes and books get collected at these stores, from where our delivery partener collects the items and some of those items are upscaled and sold again.
