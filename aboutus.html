Our Mission

We exist to disrupt the landfill economy. We believe the coolest items are the ones that already exist.

ReLove is India's leading destination for circular fashion. We bring together the best independent thrift shops and closet-cleaners across India — they hold the pieces, we handle the order — creating a sustainable loop that never ends.

EST. 2026

FROM
GARAGE
TO GALAXY

It started with two friends, a pile of acid-wash jeans, and a shared hatred for mall brands.

We realized the best clothes were already made decades ago. They were sitting in wardrobes, collecting dust, or worse—heading to the dump.

So we built Relove. A place where "second-hand" is a flex, not a compromise. A place that feels less like a transaction and more like swapping clothes with your coolest friends.
